Local moving considerations

Plan Your Downtown Dubai Move with Confidence

Downtown Dubai includes high-rise residences and mixed-use buildings. A little preparation can help reduce last-minute access issues and keep your moving day organised.

  • ✓
    Confirm service elevator access

    Ask building management whether a lift reservation is required and which time slots are available.

  • ✓
    Check permits and NOCs

    Requirements differ by building. Confirm any documents or approvals with your property management.

  • ✓
    Arrange loading and parking

    Check vehicle access, loading areas and any restrictions before the scheduled move.

  • ✓
    Measure large furniture

    Check doorways, corridors and service lifts for bulky pieces or items that may need dismantling.

Building procedures are not the same everywhere. Confirm the rules for your own building before moving day.
